## **Sam Gilliam's Unknown Pleasures**

After reading Sam Gilliam's new work, I feel that there is a "useless stone" to say the feeling of touching the stone. The painter uses wet canvas to create undulations, sometimes valley rise, sometimes adding multiple canvas layers, then randomly adding pigments and grains on top, the first sight has a very strong geographical feeling, as if a bird's eye view of a mountain scenery, fields, or desert. The whole canvas is like the surface of the earth, recording the traces of the painter "making" this "landscape". It can be seen from what corner here and in what direction this bunch of elements randomly placed on the canvas are presented like this Bend it up left and up, just enough to pocket the grains, like a nest. And when you get closer to the canvas to see, every detail has his random, "natural", and so very exciting place, slightly shift the corner here will also see another piece of landscape, another kind of beauty.

Amazing. After reading this, I really want to fall asleep. His paintings are so looking and touching!

## **20C - Elaine’s? Home?**

What is a home? Elaine walks into a new apartment with his memories, terrorizing every corner of Elaine’s stuff: “moving” footage of trying to bring history into a strange space, dried flowers refrigerated trying to suspend and archive time, old feelings trying to project warmth on the cold walls of the bathroom, memories broken in a glass music box… all the details are not about this place, time and past. Unfamiliar smells, too comfortable but never comfortable sofas, beds that have to be kept tidy and can't sit on, slippers that will always stay neatly at the head of the bed... Some spaces can't be broken no matter how much content they have, and some spaces can never be created or shaped. What is a house? Is immigration "moving" or "leaving home"? I think for this space, Elaine is a passer-by, and the audience is just a passer-by. For me, these ten minutes with Elaine in Elaine's memories made me feel strange, incompatible space and content, plus Elaine's last sentence "the next guest's time is coming", always reminded me that this space does not belong to anyone, and I seem to see, peek into the previous hand left, also misplaced memories, and the next hand, the next peeper is about to arrive. Some places and spaces are destined to be immobile.

## **Tempé Art Space Last Day**

A very favorite art space lately, (except because it is close to my house,) or because most of his exhibitions are quite to my taste, have a very pure, simple, direct feeling. Once inside, the space is not large. At a glance, there is a toilet, two windows, and a bunch of sockets and electrical pipes. And these direct presets have had discordant interactions with the works in most previous exhibitions. Although not every exhibition has a special feeling, all exhibitions are very sincere and at a glance.

Also I like this place because it is in a commercial building in Hong Kong, upstairs and downstairs are often seen children learning to draw, learn musical instruments, interest classes, etc., every time I come to this space feel very "easy", very simple. This sense of familiarity is the inherent texture of this space, and although this has not been explored in previous exhibitions, it may be the reason why this space gives me a pure feeling.

I can't bear it!

## **// Au revoir // — Petite Maman**

What should I say in a good goodbye? Before entering, I saw an interview saying that the film was inspired by Hayao Miyazaki, Mabel Normand and Aunhua Hsu, and I immediately related to "Thousand and Away" after watching it. The same magical journey that unfolds in the forest, because of a sudden parting, and the same to deal with the things to do well after and before parting. The whole journey runs through the “love” of three generations of mother and daughter Nelly, Marison and Nelly, and in this relationship, men do not need and are not eligible to participate. The journey also allows Nelly to say goodbye to her departed "loved one" once again and think about how to say the best "Au revoir".

Undertaking Portrait of a Lady on Fire, the director once again uses his best eyes to tell the love and emotion between all the relationships, of course, as a sketch, this time no very epic and poetic gazing, but the actor's every look is very real, full of love! Every action and exchange designed for the character also fully expresses the discord of each relationship, the most profound being the snack time when Nelly and Marison arrived in the car at the beginning, smiles, expressions, what to eat, how to eat, all carefully designed and love. Finally, of course, there is no shortage of soundtrack, Summer at the end of the last Portrait was very powerful, this time also continues to have Para One's soundtrack, riding the mother and daughter's final journey of exploration before saying goodbye.
