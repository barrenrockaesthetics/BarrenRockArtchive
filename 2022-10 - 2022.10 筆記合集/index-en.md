# 2022.10 Notes Collection

Exhibition:

Cosmic data receiving

Artist Artist :

Jessica FU

Venue: Oil Street Art Space

Date Date :

2022.10.10

The plan is to use the spacecraft as the main body, and stay in Oil Street with the perspective of an outsider, to study the plants and the land under your feet that you may or have not been to. Starting from a map of Oil Street redrawn in geometric language, we learn about the story of a spaceship visiting "land". We enter the spaceship and re-observe the plants on "land" and the transformed emotions in the universe pieced together using the collected images.

//Landlander Have you been to the area? //

In contrast to the surrounding cityscape in North Point, facing Oil Street, plants are the protagonists and the objects of observation in the planning. "Tree Data Collecting" can be regarded as a data room where the spacecraft can process data. The work uses a close-up lens to carefully capture the surface of plants and displays it on three vertical screens. Although the plants in the shots are from Oil Street, they are very unfamiliar to the eyes, more like looking down at the surface of a barren planet. Sleeping on a bench and immersed in three screens, for city dwellers who have long been alienated from plants, it is no different from the first "landing" of a spacecraft, observing a land that we have never had any idea of.

//Although the person is not there, the heart is where it is//

Stories are projected textually on frosted windows, stories and emotions magnified and reduced at the corners of the window frames, sometimes secured within the glass. We vaguely know that the spaceship's companion [怹] probably doesn't know this land, but Kazuki seems to be familiar with it. In the eyes of the spaceship, he ended up becoming a land man, asking the spaceship to wait and wait, unwilling to leave. The plot is all inspired by the memory of Oil Street and North Point, while watching the scene of residents playing on the grass of Oil Street behind the window, as if wearing left [怹] glasses, and watching the land "native", "tasteful" life, and watching the seemingly familiar "home". The plan is to incorporate observations of plants, spaces and memories into the “lesson of coming home,” presumably the uneasiness that just observation is bound to touch in these years.
