## **// Utopia is an empty reality, a fantasy that is too far from the ground; dystopia is a loss where everything collapses and there is no way out. //**

![image](./images/image_1.jpg)

"Landing (We talked, and then you forgot these words)" (Xu Sile, 2021)

Personally, I no longer believe that utopia can lead us forward, and I am tired of the nihilism of dystopia, as well as the optimism and nameless perseverance that most fight against dystopia. While most discussions about utopia only project imagination into the future without carrying out present-day practices and improvements, this is also the first question after entering the exhibition: "Do we live on the same planet?" - Have humans ever been in the same place and time to think about issues about the future together?

The works in "Place of Coexistence" use a lot of performative elements. Kobe said that this is an unintentional consensus between her and the artists. Taiwanese art critic Zhang Yiwen (2021) proposed that "performativity" is the formation of new systems and languages ​​through repeated performances, postures and movements (1). Performance is "living history". It is a ready-made object that inherits the physical memory and life experience carried by the performer in the past, and then displays it in a progressive manner on the spot. Each moment is the "present" that is stacked together with the "past", and the same is true for improvisation. Such present-style and experiential reflection is precisely the practice that was lacking in discussions of the future and utopia in the past.

## **Coexist with Them**

Cyborg - that is, transforming people, is a frequent visitor to utopian blueprints. Along with this comes the problem of technology that never lacks value. Human beings still seem to be unable to accept the combination of the body and metal, and they have always been afraid that technology will become "customer-oriented". Florence Lam explores the car wreckage with her body and sweat for an unlimited time and for a long time, and interacts with it (interaction? Rather than interaction, it is more like her soft body, which can only move under the restrictions of the rigid and unmoved wreckage), trying to use the most ancient writing system of "humanity" on the wreckage to establish a "post-human" civilization. "Break" means illness, bed rest, and talking to metal may still be taboo activities for people. Whether it can be extended to healing as Florence said requires more coexistence practices in reality and psychology. "疒" shows the strong contrast between the two sides in a progressive manner, demonstrating on the spot how to use human attitudes and experiences to confront the situation that post-human beings have to face in utopia and even now. Whether it succeeds or not may prove whether the accumulated knowledge from human history to this day is enough for us to have the ability to face the above problems.

## **Living with Us**

Florence attempts to establish a new language, while Xu Haolin and He Qiantong reflect on our understanding of the old language. Xu Haolin's "Sweet Iteration" refers to the endless reincarnation of nostalgic futurism in fashion, asking about the possibility of nostalgia and the whereabouts of human beings in the era. Time is as powerless as the translucent device that seals the cheongsam, unable to prevent the clothing from projecting the visual culture it contains to future generations. Culture will ignore time and continue to be stacked and updated. The work also sets up a "nostalgic" place for the audience. Under the translucent screen, viewers can naturally and safely read the artist's explanations of nostalgia, digital, past and future with their knees in their arms. They have even become a part of the work, performing the state of longing for the past and the future in a modern place.

Language is the sum of human civilization systems, and marriage is a contract that guarantees the future. In "Contract of Coexistence (Advanced, "Abandonment" to "Focus"), Ho Sian Tung reorganizes language with her partner. It is an exercise based on the present, consolidating the past, and establishing the future. In the future of "1984", humans have created Newspeak, and technology has gradually changed how humans use language. But are humans ready to face the language of the future? The "new human beings" in the animation world put forward the importance of mutual understanding. He Qiantong also regards "UNDERSTAND" as an important contract, but understanding is not achieved in a flash. The contract with her partner can be mutually affirmed, relying on their mutual interactions and efforts in the past.

## **Coexisting with Them**

Apart from matter and humans, can we still expect a future in which animals and plants coexist? Since the Industrial Revolution, humans have regarded things other than humans as things that can be used at will, but sericulture has appeared as early as BC. Years later, the silkworm children in Zhan Xianlan's "Good Laborers" still demonstrate the same instincts as their ancestors. What has been changing is the relationship between humans and them. The installation works present the most imaginable "future of silkworms" to the audience. Silkworms demonstrate the ability to be "labored by humans" under power. However, on the other hand, as an artist, Liv cannot raise silkworms industrially. Day and night care is the way she chooses to coexist with silkworms. She has also been cheering for the silkworm captain Tingting!

Huang Jixue demonstrated her interaction with the sunflowers in "Weaving a Heddle Like Life" - repeated but patient care, and the feeling of mutual care. Huang refuses to use the old method of recording daily life to display their coexistence experience, but instead places the responsibility and power of display on the posture of the sunflower. The "body" and each cell of the sunflower are the best medium to prove all this. The audience must observe as carefully as the artist can find evidence of their coexistence and common expectation for the future in the sunflower.

## **Walkthrough coexistence? **

The "present" can only be formed by stacking the "past", and the "future" can only be formed by stacking the "now". To imagine the future and prepare for the future, we must practice now. "Place of Coexistence" raises the post-human issues that humans must face. The purpose is not to get answers to the future, but to allow curators, artists, audiences, and even animals and plants to continue to learn to coexist with others in the exhibition, and each ongoing exercise will determine what kind of future we move towards. Facing an era that is about to go crazy, perhaps we should first capture the people we are living with.
