We are set to step in others’ paths. While conditions may be cramped, we can still come together clumsily in dance. That was an unpredictable world, yet freeing in its openness. We have the liberty to choose where our attention falls - drawn at times to the boisterous, at times fascinated by the subtle tugging between cables. As with most of their performative practices, we each hold a material, or an object to always interact with and fix our focus on. As we wander through this vast world, may unwavering gaze and keen senses bring us deeper understanding of the encounters that objects are guiding us to.

We are set to step in others’ paths! Let no timidness hold us back from exploring routes not our own or avoiding unfamiliar sights. When ready, we might connect through simple gestures - touching each other's palms, inscribing dreams on a soaked bread, or dropping blessings into the barely cup-sized fountain. Amid chaos, appreciating each nuanced feeling brings richness. Only with the subtle yet solid ones will the community be diverse and complete. I recalled someone comparing Char Siu Rice (barbecue pork rice) with chords: rice serves as the foundation, the lesser pork plays a lively role, but they also rely on the rarer soy sauce for seasoning too, and not to forget the onions on top that shake subtly like the weird 7th in chords. Perhaps this is how harmony works, with every single element valued, having highs and lows. May we proactively witness the happenings between materials, bodies and emotions, notice subtle loneliness, and accompany both those who want and do not want to be heard.

Happy to see per.platform having grown into a much larger community. Thank you for their performance alongside Stilllive ​​that enlightens and inspires. Best wishes for Current Plans and @eeunicee for their next phase of evolving work.
