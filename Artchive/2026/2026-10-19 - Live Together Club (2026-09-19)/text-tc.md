### *PERMANENTLY UNLANDED* (2026) by Ice Wong

*Section 1: wrapped in It’s been good (Graits!)*

[自然地用左英文寫] Unpack - a box like luggage. Carefully detach the stickers, not to break any. Preserve the possibility to repackage it. A rectangular suitcase. Adjust your body to unpack, because the case is more important than yours. Tired stickers, marked the edge of the fold embedded. Large paper, so large that it gives confidence for wrapping anything in.

The large paper unfolded. It is really a suitcase, but unthought to be having the same color of her top. Stickers are reused, to stabilize the square wrapping paper on floor. It is a new suitcase, is it? The wrapping (another) is still on the suitcase. But there are barcodes (for checked baggage?) Oh the wrap is sticked together by A4 papers.

*Section 2: (adapted version)*

Ride a suitcase, to escape the square? Push me back, land me anywhere. Unsettling sounds, there is a green lemonade ceramic bowl inside, with a tiny cute handle. Push me back, there is milktea cup inside. The suitcase is bending, there is another sharing cup (?). The paper starts to wrinkle, there is another dark navy cup. Why would you keep leaving the square and ask for help? That is intentional you may know. This time it is a sky blue one. Safety is the first priority. This time, we landed somewhere outside. It is a cup with trees printed on it, and we still have a tiny wrapping paper from the cup. Another one landed outside, it is a complete white one. Socks are clumsy. Barefoot is great. The cup needs a bigger handle. Sometimes we need to fix the orientation of the suitcase. This time you chose to leave with it.

*Section 3: “But it feels like your second home, right?” She asked.*

A milk tea stand. Vita, Lipton, Fairwood, they are the Hongkongese right? Two teabags per cup. “Tlick” is the boiling sound when you look for boiling water outside home. 黑白淡奶, accompanied best by teaspoon, easy and pragmatic. Half pack of white sugar from Cafe de Carol ……
