What is the essence of understanding the shapes of birds? Hong Kong artist Ivy Ma has been on her artistic journey across the globe for a while now. She has minimised her practice to crafting paperworks. Each piece in Bird of Shape (no. 216 - no. 575) begins with two continuous snapshots of flying birds printed on an A4 sheet. Despite the proximity of the captured moments, the birds' swift movements reveal temporal and spatial gaps between the photos. Ivy's artistry comes alive in the negative spaces she cuts, marks, and collages. Wherever the birds soared, she transformed them into seas of golden light, reminiscent of Carol's All that is Solid Melts into Air, with the golden shreds weaving across frames and time. In Ivy’s work, photographs are transformed into paintings and even sculptures.

Trace the cutting edges, find where the artist began to shape the birds. Those delicate, easily overlooked edges exist firm, almost as thin as the thread in Man Meito’s sculpture, Diffusing Linear Body. These cutting edges starkly contrast the golden shreds the birds flew through. Can actions traverse time and space to create meaning? And if they can, are they thin, fine threads, or golden spaces waiting to be created?

Humans cannot soar like birds, always searching for a place to land. June's piece, The Light Is Still On, paints a motion picture of city dwelling with brushstrokes and Cantonese, hinting at an underlying depression without pointing to a specific event. Yet, as a Hongkonger, standing in Catalonia and facing June's work, I can't help but recall the collective trauma—the believed driving force behind many of us taking flight and moving forward. As you fly, have you truly made progress with each step, with each moment?

P.S. Jamsen openly shares his hopes for Non-Solo and nProjekt to connect Hong Kong's art scene with the global stage. After visiting the Museum of the History of Catalonia and then Bird of Shape, I found myself grappling with this distant path. Ivy Ma noted, "I yearn to seek a shape beyond homeland." But does seeking "beyond homeland" require the existence of a homeland? How will it lead us to navigate the international stage, which often demands positionality? Or if we put intentions aside, where will the path of "beyond homeland" eventually lead us?
