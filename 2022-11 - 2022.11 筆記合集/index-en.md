## **Not as good as you think, but not as bad either**

Following the last time "The 333rd Day I Left Home", Wang Shunyu once again depicts the spiritual outlook of a stranger. This time it focuses on how Shen Piao thinks about "roots" and "home". Harvest and stay are the struggles that everyone knows that deep drifting will have. "What have you gained after ten years of deep drifting?" a house. "(2022), the house is a bunch of confused flowers whose outline cannot be grasped; "When do you plan to leave Shenzhen? "(2022) prays for a white rabbit that can only take them into dreamland. I have always been fascinated by Wang Shunyu's paintings and the chemical effect between their titles. This time, the text poses a dilemma, and the images reflect the unspeakable answers in the protagonist's heart.

What other emotions can we have living in a city where we can only be uncertain and unable to grasp our roots? Even though they tried their best to bring a smile, everything was reflected in those faces that were forced to twist. This work also features a close-up of the facial expressions of the protagonists of the story. Not only the protagonists, but also the protagonist's fish are stunned. The pace of the metropolis is so fast that it is nowhere to be found, emotions are nakedly stagnated on their faces, their faces remind me of social media memes, and can only face the powerless world with absurd faces - "Cruelty: Not only Shenzhen, in fact, you go to any first-tier city like this, working overtime, making money, commuting; so, come on. 》—Shenzhen is like this, Hong Kong is like this, in Hong Kong, we may call these "only Jack-like looks" or "Lunar New Year's Day looks like".

But ""Human Pain Is Not Connected"", in addition to the left face, the painting also finds "tree holes" in the plants (Wang Shunyu's last solo exhibition of the plate planting, also documented the longing for relatives and the sorrow in it). The plants are vaguely represented on silk, with no torso and spiritual bodies left. Although everything became unbearable, the final white peony still glowed - "It's not as beautiful as you think, but it's not as bad either." I think, whether you are drifting or not, whether you are in a first-tier city or not, now, everyone can only say that.
