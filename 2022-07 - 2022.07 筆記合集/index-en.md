The sun at midnight is probably located on the other side of the earth. Do we expect a little bit of sunlight to pass through the soil, crust and core, and come to our feet, shining a ray of light in the dark night?

Probably everyone is trying to capture the only light and shadow in the dark night, The Imbalanced Years' images of light and shadow, time, constraints and pain on the walls hint at our current situation, but what is more powerful is the installation part made of images and chairs in the work. The image is projected from under one chair to the other cut in half. The shadow of the half chair is projected onto the wall. The photo of the whole chair is printed on the wall. The wall foot on the photo matches the position of the wall foot of the venue. This series of operations throughout the image and physical, overlapping "chairs" inevitably does not remind people of how Joseph Kosuth's One and Three Chairs depicts the concept of Chair, but the Chair is not only one Chair, but also three chairs. "The Unbalanced Years" deliberately "strings" all the "chairs" on the same axis, but it happens to reflect so many objects in the space of discord - some with us in the three-dimensional space, some in the shadow, some on the photo... Even if it can be a hair to move the whole body, they are always separated within the discord.

“Instinct” places small hands shaped in graphite on a crib, the shape of the hand showing a desire to catch something. He could not catch the warm light that fell from heaven, and the shadow of the fence of the bed fell upon his palm; A similar contrasting state can also be found in the details of the work: pulling up a bed made of wire mesh by fishing line, connecting wooden poles with thin rows of nails, supporting harder-looking hands with pillows, and so on. The work raises desires between the struggles, and then only catches the shadow, not the light, staying in a state of unknown failure or not, extending the powerlessness of "The Years of Unbalanced".

In addition to the works above on the left, other works and samples constantly wander between light and shadow, including using projection to create silhouettes with light, using mezzotint to "draw" light in the dark, using film to collect sunlight to create light and shadow "painting" images, processing words and images with "black" on "white", etc... Using different media, I try to explore all the skills of "making light", "leaving light", "making shadow", and "leaving shadow". Artists "equip" themselves with creation in life. Whether they are looking for the sun in the middle of the night or living in the darkness of noon, they all decide to insist on having the power to bend and stretch and let themselves breathe.
