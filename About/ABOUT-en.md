# ABOUT

### Aesthetics

*Barren Rock* takes its name from a conversation with a friend. He believes "beauty" is "one lump of stuff", and that making art is like the story of the blind men feeling the elephant, with everything they touch coming from the same "beauty".

I don't think he means an Ideal Form in the Platonic sense. Rather, "beauty" itself is one enormous object, and each of us only ever touches a part of it.

I cannot decide if all "beauty" shares a single source. "Beauty" might be a giant rock? Maybe. But the elephant analogy appealed to me. The work of art is to record the nuances of the rock through our bodies and touches, then replicate them in our livinig world, as "beauty".

How to touch the rock, and what comes of touching it, is probably what art criticism and aesthetics should advocate.

### Barren Rock

When it comes to rocks, the first thing that comes to my mind is "Barren Rock".

They said, "Barren Rock" was the first words British used to describe Hong Kong: a place where not a blade of grass grew. I cannot decide whether this "rock" is now fertile or not, but what is certain is that it hardly counts as desolate.

Whether it is "barren" or not, I guess, depends very much on your way of seeing it.
